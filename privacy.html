ParkAce Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

ParkAce is a theme-park companion app that helps you find your way around the park, see ride wait times, and plan your day. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. ParkAce has no user accounts and never asks for your name, email, or login.

Information we handle

Location

With your permission, ParkAce uses your device's location only while the app is open to show where you are in the park and to give you walking directions between attractions. Your location is processed on your device and is not stored on our servers in a way that identifies you.

If you turn on crowd-data sharing (optional, off unless you enable it), the app contributes anonymous crowd observations: your position is reduced to a coarse map-grid cell and a rounded time window, never your precise path, and is grouped only with a random device identifier (described below) so we can estimate how busy areas are. You can turn this off at any time in the app's settings. If you deny or disable location access entirely, ParkAce still works in a limited mode (you can pick a starting point manually).

A random device identifier

To deliver wait-drop alerts and live-activity updates for the rides you choose to watch, the app generates a random identifier when you first launch it and stores related preferences under that identifier. This identifier is not Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) or identifier for vendors (IDFV), and it is not tied to your name, email, or any account. It is a pseudonym that resets if you delete and reinstall the app.

Notifications

If you enable notifications (for example, "let me know when this ride's wait drops"), we store a push-notification token associated with the random identifier so we can send those alerts. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

Analytics and crash diagnostics

ParkAce uses Google Firebase to understand how the app is used and to diagnose crashes, so we can make it better and more reliable. This includes:

  • Usage data — anonymous, aggregated events about which features are used (for example, opening the day planner).
  • Diagnostics — crash reports and performance information.
  • Approximate location — inferred by the analytics service from your network (IP) address, at city level.
  • App-instance and device identifiers used by these services to group events.

ParkAce does not use advertising, does not include an advertising identifier, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Feedback you send us

If you choose to send feedback from within the app, we receive your message, an optional screenshot you choose to attach, and basic context to help us reproduce the issue (the screen you were on, your approximate location at that moment, and app/device version information), associated with the random identifier. We use this only to respond to and act on your feedback.

How long we keep it

Server-side records expire automatically:

  • Anonymous crowd observations: a few days.
  • Watch lists and notification preferences: removed after a period of inactivity (about 90 days) or when you reinstall the app.
  • Feedback reports: about 90 days.

Who processes data for us

  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging, and App Check) provides our backend, analytics, crash reporting, notifications, and abuse protection.
  • themeparks.wiki provides public ride wait times and park schedules. We request this public data only; we do not send your personal information to it.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Your choices

  • Allow or deny location access, and turn crowd-data sharing on or off.
  • Enable or disable notifications in iOS Settings.
  • Reset the random identifier by deleting and reinstalling the app.

Children

ParkAce is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date.
