ParkAce Support

Last updated: June 6, 2026

ParkAce is a companion app for Disneyland Resort (Disneyland Park and Disney California Adventure): in-park walking directions, live ride wait times, and a day planner. Need help, found a bug, or have a suggestion? We'd love to hear from you.

Contact us: krjeffery@proton.me
We aim to reply within a few business days. The fastest way to report an in-app issue is the Feedback button inside the app (in beta builds), which attaches helpful context automatically.

Frequently asked questions

The map doesn't show my location.

ParkAce needs location permission (While Using the App) to show your position and give live directions. Enable it in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → ParkAce. Even without location, you can plan routes manually by choosing a starting point.

Wait times look wrong or are missing.

Wait times and schedules come from public theme-park data and can be delayed, sparse, or unavailable outside park operating hours. Walking directions work regardless.

I'm not getting notifications.

Make sure notifications are enabled in iOS Settings → Notifications → ParkAce, then tap a ride to "watch" it so we know which alerts to send. Notifications are optional.

Which parks are supported?

ParkAce currently supports Disneyland Park and Disney California Adventure. We hope to add more parks over time.

How is my data handled?

ParkAce requires no account and collects no personally identifying information. See our Privacy Policy for details.
